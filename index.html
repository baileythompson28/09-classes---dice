Scorecard:

Roll the dice to begin.

How To Play:

Explaining the catagories:

- 1's, 2's, 3's, 4's, 5's, 6's: sums of all rolled.

- Three of a Kind: score is the sum of all five dice.

- Four of a Kind: score is the sum of all five dice.

- Full House: fixed score of 25 points.

- Small Straight: fixed score of 30 points.

- Large Straight: fixed score of 40 points.

- Yahtzee: first Yahtzee scores 50 points.

- Additional Yahtzee: 100-point bonus if the first Yahtzee has already been scored.

- Chance: any combination; score is the sum of all five dice, used as a fallback.
